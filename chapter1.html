The Snowstorm — Year 805

Somewhere in the storm, across the snow-covered lands of Eldoria.

A hooded figure walks alone through the snow-covered lands of Eldoria. After leaving her position as an Authority, Reina Tomoe wanders through the raging blizzard.

Her weakened Abyssal powers prevent her from blinking away from the storm. She continues walking, enduring the biting cold as snow sweeps past her. Her Abyssal powers have weakened considerably.

She can no longer blink or dash away from the storm. Her legs tremble with every step. Her breathing grows weaker.

Eventually, she can no longer keep herself standing.

* * *

Reina collapses onto the snow.

She slowly lifts her gaze toward the stormy sky, struggling to remain conscious.

Reina

“Will... this... change my life...? Leaving my... title as an Authority... Deishreth... I can't... die here...”

Several seconds pass. Then, a strange vibration echoes through the snow. The snow begins to shift, slowly moving aside as someone approaches her.

A figure emerges through the blizzard.

It is King Deishreth.

He has found her.

Deishreth

“Found you...”

Reina

“So... *faint cough*... you found me...”

She manages a faint smirk and smile before losing consciousness.

Deishreth

“Sorry for being late, my love... You can rest while I bash my way through this storm and get us out of here...”

Ostraea does not directly order Reina's death. Instead, she leaves the decision to Deisha, making it feel more like a judgment being permitted than an execution being commanded.

Deishreth

The next day, Reina remains in a deep sleep as her body continues recovering. Within the depths of her dream, something strange begins to unfold.

A faint image appears.

The Ruins of Eldoria

The battlefield is devastated. Debris lies everywhere, and the surrounding houses have been severely damaged.

Reina is gravely wounded. Her strength has nearly vanished after attempting to fight back against Deisha, the Authority of Dark.

Nearby, young Jin cries beside his dying mother. Rin has already been abducted by Deisha.

Jin

Jin desperately calls for help as Reina struggles to remain conscious. The sound of approaching horses grows louder. King Deishreth finally arrives.

The image blurs. Their final conversation can barely be heard.

Deishreth

“You got it...”

Deishreth

“Someday... I will take you...”

Reina's vision begins to fade. She can barely hear him, and her body stops responding before he can finish his sentence.

Deishreth continues speaking.

Deishreth

“...to a place we always talk about...”

Reina dies peacefully, smiling at Deishreth. Young Jin continues crying, desperately trying to reach his mother. Deishreth closes his eyes, tears faintly streaming down his face.

Deishreth

“L-Let me finish it... Damn it...”

The image blurs and fades away.
